Trust Intelligence Framework

Four levels, each earned

Every clinic starts at Listed and moves up as we review more. Badges describe the documents and behaviour we checked. They do not rate the quality of medical care.

Level 1

Listed

  • Name, area and contact details collected
  • Not yet reviewed by us
Level 2

Verified

  • Registration and licence documents reviewed
  • Location and phone confirmed
Level 3

Trusted

  • Verified, plus daily updates kept up over time
  • Patient feedback reviewed
Level 4

Certified

  • Planned for later
  • Needs a partner, such as a professional body

How a clinic moves up

1

The clinic applies

From its console, a clinic submits its registration number and licence reference for the level it wants.

2

We review

A person on the Front Belt team checks the documents against the registration and licence records.

3

The badge changes

When the team sets the new level, the badge on the public site updates within seconds — no redeploy, no waiting.

4

It can also come down

If a licence lapses or updates stop, the level drops and the listing says why.

If a clinic stops updating

  • 1 missed day: shows “Not updated today”
  • 3 missed days: shows “Status unknown” and drops in search
  • 7 missed days: hidden until the clinic updates

What we will not do

  • Sell trust badges
  • Show invented reviews or ratings
  • Claim a clinic is safe or high quality

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Front Belt live yet?

Not fully. We are in early access and adding real clinics one at a time. Listings marked “Sample” are demonstrations.

Does a badge mean the clinic gives good care?

No. A badge shows which documents and behaviours we have reviewed, such as registration and daily updates. It is not a medical quality rating.

Do I have to create an account to search?

No. Searching, distance sorting and the map all work without signing in. An account only adds saved places.

Is it an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call your local emergency number.

Does it cost anything?

Searching is free for patients.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing in Phase 1. We may later offer optional paid features such as featured placement. Trust badges will never be for sale.

Can I change my own listing?

Yes. Your provider console lets you edit your details, coordinates, hours and services, and update today's status whenever it changes.

Can I upgrade my own trust level?

You can apply for it with your documents, but only the Front Belt team can grant it. That is deliberate: if clinics could upgrade themselves, the badge would mean nothing.

How do I remove my clinic from the listing?

Use “Hide my listing” in your console — it comes off the public site immediately and your data stays for when you return. The Front Belt team can delete a listing completely on request.

How long does the daily update take?

Under a minute: open or closed, expected wait and how busy you are.

What documents do you need?

Your clinic registration and the licences that apply to your services. The console asks for the registration number and a document reference.
