How it works

Three things you can check in under a minute

Open or closed today

The clinic confirms its status each morning, including public holidays and unplanned closures.

How busy it is

Expected wait and a simple busyness bar, so you can choose a quieter time or a nearby clinic.

How much we have checked

A trust level shows what we have actually reviewed. No clinic can buy one.

Trust Intelligence Framework

Four levels, each earned

Every clinic starts at Listed and moves up as we review more. Badges describe the documents and behaviour we checked. They do not rate the quality of medical care.

Level 1

Listed

  • Name, area and contact details collected
  • Not yet reviewed by us
Level 2

Verified

  • Registration and licence documents reviewed
  • Location and phone confirmed
Level 3

Trusted

  • Verified, plus daily updates kept up over time
  • Patient feedback reviewed
Level 4

Certified

  • Planned for later
  • Needs a partner, such as a professional body

If a clinic stops updating

  • 1 missed day: shows “Not updated today”
  • 3 missed days: shows “Status unknown” and drops in search
  • 7 missed days: hidden until the clinic updates

What we will not do

  • Sell trust badges
  • Show invented reviews or ratings
  • Claim a clinic is safe or high quality

Why Front Belt

Built by someone who has mapped how far care is

Front Belt was started by a solo founder in Nairobi with a GIS background. An earlier project mapped healthcare accessibility in Matungu Subcounty, Kakamega County, and showed how often the hardest part of getting care is simply knowing where to go and whether it is open.

We are starting with clinics in Nairobi because that is a problem we can solve well, and prove, before we take on anything else.

Questions

FAQ

Is Front Belt live yet?

Not fully. We are in early access and adding real clinics one at a time. The listings on this page are samples.

Does a badge mean the clinic gives good care?

No. A badge shows which documents and behaviours we have reviewed, such as registration and daily updates. It is not a medical quality rating.

Is it an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call your local emergency number.

Does it cost anything?

Searching is free for patients.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing in Phase 1. We may later offer optional paid features such as featured placement. Trust badges will never be for sale.

How long does the daily update take?

It is meant to take under a minute: open or closed, expected wait and how busy you are.

What documents do you need?

Your clinic registration and the licences that apply to your services. We will tell you exactly what when you reach out.

What if we miss a few days?

Your card shows “Not updated today”, then “Status unknown”, and after seven days it is hidden until you update again.
