Trust Intelligence Framework

Four levels, each earned

Every clinic starts at Listed and moves up as we review more. Badges describe the documents and behaviour we checked. They do not rate the quality of medical care.

Level 1

Listed

  • Name, area and contact details collected
  • Not yet reviewed by us
Level 2

Verified

  • Registration and licence documents reviewed
  • Location and phone confirmed
Level 3

Trusted

  • Verified, plus daily updates kept up over time
  • Patient feedback reviewed
Level 4

Certified

  • Planned for later
  • Needs a partner, such as a professional body

How a clinic moves up

1

The clinic applies

From its console, a clinic submits its registration number and licence reference for the level it wants.

2

We review

A person on the Front Belt team checks the documents against the registration and licence records.

3

The badge changes

When the team sets the new level, the badge on the public site updates within seconds — no redeploy, no waiting.

4

It can also come down

If a licence lapses or updates stop, the level drops and the listing says why.

If a clinic stops updating

  • 1 missed day: shows “Not updated today”
  • 3 missed days: shows “Status unknown” and drops in search
  • 7 missed days: hidden until the clinic updates

What we will not do

  • Sell trust badges
  • Show invented reviews or ratings
  • Claim a clinic is safe or high quality

How a listing gets approved

1

You submit

Create a partner account and fill in your details: name, category, the exact pin on the map, phone, WhatsApp, hours and what you offer. About three minutes.

2

You see it received

Immediately after submitting, your console shows a status tracker: Received → In review → Decision. Come back any time to see where it stands.

3

We review it

A person on the Front Belt team checks the pin, the phone number, the name and the category. We aim to answer within 2 working days.

4

Live — or told why not

Approved listings appear in search immediately. If we cannot approve it, your console lists exactly which requirement was not met, so you fix it and resubmit.

About Front Belt

Not another list of phone numbers

Front Belt is a Nairobi-only place finder. One search covers the seven things a person actually needs nearby — health, food, beauty, tech and money, books, home and accommodation — and every answer is built for choosing, not just browsing.

Connect, Compare & Access

Each wedge in the mark is one category. The belt is the road that runs past all of them — which is where the name comes from.

What actually makes it different

Maintained, not scraped

The business itself keeps the listing current, and every listing shows the date it was last confirmed. A directory nobody maintains is just an old phone book — that is the whole reason for the review queue.

Built for the decision

Every place is described the same way — distance, hours, price band, services, and for clinics the wait. Same fields, side by side, so comparing takes seconds instead of six apps.

Wait times, not guesses

Only Health carries a daily status, and it comes from the clinic itself. If a clinic stops updating, the site says so instead of pretending — and after a week the listing steps aside.

Straight to the business

Call, WhatsApp or Start — your tap goes to the business, not through us. No booking fee on a visit, no middleman holding the number, no "send a DM and wait".

Open map, open data

The map is OpenStreetMap, not Google — so there is no map bill and no licence to lose. Our team also finds missing businesses in the open map data, then phones them before anything goes live.

Nothing sold that shouldn't be

Trust levels are earned and never bought, in any category. No invented reviews, no paid ranking dressed up as a result. A badge answers one question honestly: what did Front Belt actually check?

What we are not

  • Not a review site. We do not invent ratings, and we do not rank care.
  • Not an ad feed. Paid placement is labelled and capped, and it never touches Health ranking.
  • Not a middleman. We do not sit between you and the business taking a cut of the visit.
  • Not nationwide (yet). Nairobi first, done properly, before anywhere else.

Who we are

Front Belt Technologies is a small team in Nairobi building this in the open. The directory is in early access: listings are added and reviewed one at a time, and every category is free while we prove it works.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Front Belt live yet?

Not fully. We are in early access and adding real clinics one at a time. Listings marked “Sample” are demonstrations.

Does a badge mean the clinic gives good care?

No. A badge shows which documents and behaviours we have reviewed, such as registration and daily updates. It is not a medical quality rating.

Do I have to create an account to search?

No. Searching, distance sorting and the map all work without signing in. An account only adds saved places.

Is it an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call your local emergency number.

Does it cost anything?

Searching is free for patients.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing in Phase 1. We may later offer optional paid features such as featured placement. Trust badges will never be for sale.

How long does approval take, and what if I am turned down?

We aim for two working days. Your console shows the status the whole time — received, in review, then the decision. If a listing is not approved you see the exact requirement that was not met, fix it, and resubmit.

Can I change my own listing?

Yes. Your partner console lets you edit your details, pin, hours and services, and update today's status whenever it changes. Changes to an approved listing go back for a quick re-check.

Can I upgrade my own trust level?

You can apply for it with your documents, but only the Front Belt team can grant it. That is deliberate: if clinics could upgrade themselves, the badge would mean nothing.

How do I remove my business from the listing?

Use “Hide my listing” in your console — it comes off the public site immediately and your data stays for when you return. The Front Belt team can delete a listing completely on request.

How long does the daily update take?

Under a minute: open or closed, expected wait and how busy you are.

What documents do you need?

Health businesses: your facility registration and the licences that apply to your services. Other categories: no documents at first — we confirm the pin and that the phone number works.
